# Autoscroller privacy policy

Autoscroller does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking, and makes no external network requests.

## Information used on your device

The extension reads the active page's scroll position and dimensions to scroll the page and detect its bottom. It adds a Stop button while scrolling. It does not collect page text, browsing history, or page addresses.

Your selected scroll speed is saved in the browser's local extension storage. It is not synced by this extension or sent to a server. Temporary scrolling state is held in each page and is reset on navigation. Uninstalling the extension removes its stored preference.

## Permissions

- **activeTab:** temporarily access the tab where you invoke the extension.
- **scripting:** run the scrolling controls and display the Stop button on that page.
- **storage:** remember your preferred speed locally.

## Support and changes

Questions can be submitted through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/khantthuaung/autoscroller/issues). Information you voluntarily post there is handled by GitHub and may be public; avoid including private information.

Changes to this policy will be published in this repository alongside the corresponding extension changes.
